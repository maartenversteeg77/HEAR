Pop-up at ADE: a skate hall turned into a dancefloor for one night

THE BRIEF

During Amsterdam Dance Event, a skate hall was converted into a dancefloor for a one-off pop-up event. The brief had two sides: give the venue a new function and atmosphere for one night, and at the same time deliver a reliable technical setup for the event and the French DJ Rivo.

EXPERIENCE

The setup consisted of rider-proof DJ gear, a professional mixer, an RCF front-of-house system and separate monitor speakers. Battery-powered lighting accentuated the DJ booth and coloured the surroundings. HEAR took care of transport, installation and technical support.

RESULT

The artist's team reported that hardly any soundcheck was needed: it sounded right straight away and the DJs were happy with the monitoring. They also appreciated the proactive communication beforehand about the player updates and the required update of the DJ's hard drive.
