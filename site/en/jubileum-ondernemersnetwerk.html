Five years of CreativeGrounds: a luxurious garden party in Amsterdam's Oud-Zuid

THE BRIEF

CreativeGrounds wanted to throw a luxurious garden party in the middle of a residential neighbourhood in Amsterdam-Zuid. With good sound for the DJs and the saxophonist, and as little impact on the neighbours as possible.

APPROACH

We used powerful short-throw systems that don't carry far. During the party we measured the sound level regularly. Light, sound and the latest DJ gear supported the programme.

RESULT

Speeches, DJs and musicians flowed seamlessly into one another. The guests were dancing all night, without causing excessive disturbance to the neighbourhood. The equipment blended in the entire look & feel.
