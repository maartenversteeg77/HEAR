Dancefloor lighting

A hybrid moving head projects beam, spot and wash in one. When choosing the right device (not a jack of all trades that excels at nothing) this fixture can be like a Swiss army knife. You get from one fixture what would otherwise take three, and with a limited number you create a show with nearly limitless different styles and atmospheres. The fixtures also change role as a night progresses: calm as a wash during the warm-up, high-impact as a beam at peak hour.

Request a proposal→or send an email

WHAT COMES WITH IT

A light haze makes the beams visible. Light columns and mirror balls add depth and give a nod to disco. And the battery wash lights from our ambient lighting offering colour the walls too. Together it forms one picture that moves with the night.

HOW WE PROGRAMME

If desired by the client, we programme a show in advance that suits his or her event. We build scenes that follow the course of the night: restrained at the opening, fuller once the dancefloor fills up. The lighting desk sits by the booth, so light and music follow each other. We use wireless controls wherever possible, so you see no or limited cable ducts across the room.
