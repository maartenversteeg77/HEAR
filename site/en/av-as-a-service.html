GUIDED PURCHASING

A sound upgrade to meet increasing client expectations

Some venues are trying to meet the rising expectations that visitors have of sound. The rise of high-end listening bars and clubs in cities around Europe raises customer expectations. The sound needs to have impact, but must not harm the ears. Or the sound isn't felt enough, or it is shrill and tiring, or there are dead zones, so the audience keeps wandering in search of a good spot.

With the owner, we look at what the venue needs. Brand-independent, with the venue’s characteristics as the starting point and a system that staff can operate themselves as the desired end point.

AV AS A SERVICE

The quality of your own installation, without the purchase.

With AV as a service you don't buy lighting and sound, you use it as a service, through HEAR itself or through our network. For a fixed monthly fee and a minimum term of 36 months, the venue gains access to the latest sound and light systems on the market, without worrying about maintenance or repairs.

After training, you operate the equipment yourself. For installation and maintenance we involve product specialists of the chosen brands, if needed.
