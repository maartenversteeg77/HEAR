Restored lighting effects from the disco era, with present-day light output

Some lighting effects were never surpassed, only forgotten. We bring back the classics from the 1990s clubs, restore them and give them a modern light source. That turns an 'oh I remember' effect into an 'oh wow' effect.

Use only vintage effects and your show looks dated. Use them to complement a light show and you get a nice mix of old and new, with effects that are different from the rest.
