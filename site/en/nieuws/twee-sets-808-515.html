New in: two sets of RCF SUB 808-AS with KX 515-A

Two compact sub-top sets for several uses

We have added two sets to our range: the RCF SUB 808-AS with the KX 515-A on top. It is our most compact set, but don't be fooled by their size. With 2,000 W of built-in amplification per top and 2,000 W per sub, the manufacturer means business.

Suitable for

  • Presentations
  • Intimate (cocktail) parties
  • DJ monitoring, left and right of the booth
  • Front fill or delay in a larger audio set

As a complete set they suit a range of uses, from presentations to garden parties and cocktail parties of up to around 150 people.

The 515 top is also an ideal front fill in a larger system. With 100° × 70° coverage, it gives hi-fi’ish sound for the first few metres of the audience.

Using the KX 515-A as front fill makes it possible to fly its big brother, the KXL 4-A, higher and aim it further back. As the KX 515-A weighs under 10 kg, it is also a practical delay or fill to improve intelligibility at the back of the room, without heavy lifting.
