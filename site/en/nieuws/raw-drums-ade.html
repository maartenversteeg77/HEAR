ADE | HEAR colours Raw Drums in dramatic reds

When you think of Dance Event, you might think of large-scale dance events: in the renovated Gashouder, in the Ziggo Dome and in the Johan Cruijff ArenA, where 40,000 people came together for David Guetta, Armin van Buuren and Afrojack.

But the real magic happens during the day at workshops and conferences, and then at night at some 1,300 smaller, intimate events scattered around the city. That's where new partnerships form, and where new talent and future stars play for their growing communities.

One of those events was Raw Drums, by Melandru, a Dutch DJ and live percussionist who brought a blend of UK garage/bass house and live percussion together with other percussionists.

They transformed the dancefloor into a unique, rhythmic experience where people are not watching the show or the DJ, but are part of an interactive experience. A man-machine sound of rolling basslines from computers and warm hand drums played by humans.

To heighten the drama, HEAR supplied its atmospheric washlights, creating a red light zone in Amsterdam, not to be confused with the harsh street life, but one of warmth, depth and community. Exactly what ADE is all about.
