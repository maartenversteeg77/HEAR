New in the booth: RCF KXM 25-A floor monitor

We have added a new speaker to our range: the RCF KXM 25-A. It is a 15-inch coaxial floor monitor for DJs who prefer their monitoring out of the audience's sight. Powerful, punchy and coherent.

What it is

The KXM 25-A is a wedge: a low, angled monitor that sits on the booth floor and points up at the DJ. The audience doesn't see it, the DJ hears themselves clearly. With 137 dB and a range from 50 Hz, it stays clear on a loud dancefloor too.

The woofer and the horn are coaxial, on one axis. So the sound comes from a single point and stays coherent, even up close. That's exactly what you want in a DJ booth.

From now on the KXM 25-A is the second monitoring option in our booth, alongside the compact sub-top combination of SUB 808-AS and KX 515-A.
