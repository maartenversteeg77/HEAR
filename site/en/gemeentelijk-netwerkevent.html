Business & pleasure: from in-depth seminars to a cocktail hour with DJ

THE BRIEF

A municipal organisation in The Hague holds a large networking event every year around a social theme. This time the focus was on the future of young people: how do you offer them prospects and help prevent them from drifting into crime? Around 500 guests took part in lectures and workshops, followed by a networking evening.

EXPERIENCE

HEAR provided light and sound for a programme that combined content and encounter. Wireless lighting kept the look clean and avoided cables on the floor. After a series of lectures and workshops for some 500 guests there was a cocktail hour: a few hours in which the audience moved seamlessly from a substantive event to a convivial networking drinks reception.

RESULT

The client appreciated the attention to detail and the polished look. With wireless lighting, and no cables to trip over. The music was modern yet accessible.
