Private party for 150 guests: from ordinary room to colourful dancefloor

THE BRIEF

With HEAR, Maarten mainly works on dance events and corporate events. For Ronald Pronk he also took care of a private event for 150 friends and relations. Maarten and Ronald have known each other as DJs for a long time.

EXPERIENCE

For the event, the room was transformed into a colourful dancefloor for several DJs. With Ronald and other DJs in his network, Maarten shares knowledge and experience about the things you run into as a DJ behind the scenes.

RESULT

For one night, the space became a lively, colourful dancefloor for 150 friends and relations, with several DJs.
