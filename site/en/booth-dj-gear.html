A complete, rider-proof DJ set-up with booth, players, mixer and monitoring

A DJ who walks in and can get straight to work. That's the idea behind our booth. With the best DJ gear, proper monitoring and enough room to work and for storage.

Request a proposal→or ask your question on WhatsApp

HOW WE WORK

Before the event, we discuss the rider and the set-up with the DJ or his/her management. Our standard booth offers room for three networked CDJ-3000X media players and a mixer of choice: the Pioneer DJ V10 or the AlphaTheta Euphonia. For limited spaces there is also an Ecler WARM2 available. Additional requests are possible, with an alternative booth if needed.

MONITORING

The DJ can choose between a compact sub-top combination (RCF SUB 808-AS + KX 515-A) with the tops at ear height, placed left and right. Or wedge-shaped floor monitors (KXM 25-A), invisible to the audience and aimed up at the DJ.

THE BOOTH

A two-metre oval unit in dark grey, with an illuminated front that changes colour with the night. The technology is built in: network hub, cabling and power stay out of sight.

HOUSE RULES

  • No redlining :-)
  • We expect the DJ and entourage to make sure no drinks are put on the booth or handed across it. An accident can damage not only the gear, but also the event itself, for example through a short circuit.
  • Bring memory drives with the latest version of Rekordbox, or at least with dual file management. If in doubt or if you have any questions, please get in touch early.
  • Please don't turn the booth into a cloakroom for guests ;-)
