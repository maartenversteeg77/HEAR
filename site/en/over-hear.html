Founder story

OVER HEAR

I'm Maarten. Since 1993 I've been DJing in bars, clubs, rooftop bars and beach clubs, and at corporate events from fashion shows to product launches. Sometimes abroad. Along the way I've come to know a lot of different rooms and a lot of light and sound systems. Sometimes it sounds great. Sometimes you hear what's still possible.

Alongside that, I've worked for some 25 years as a communications consultant for large organisations. So I also know how much a brand activation, product launch or staff event matters to a client.

HEAR grew out of that combination. I start from what a client, agency or artist has in mind, and find the sound and light to match. As a DJ, I hear what a room needs. As a consultant, I understand what the client wants to achieve.

Social media are changing music and the DJ profession. Events are getting bigger, and sometimes the show seems to matter more than the artist. At HEAR, light and sound serve the artist, with colourful, creative light shows that strengthen the music and the atmosphere.

I do this at small to medium-sized events: from 100 to 1,500 people, in venues up to 40 metres deep. I work with a network of experienced freelance professionals.

I also help venue owners turn their space into a dancefloor for a one-off event. Or I advise them on buying their own light and sound: what does the room need, what's possible, and how do you make sure it works well, needs little maintenance and lasts? And I put them in touch with the right suppliers.

The name HEAR has two meanings. First of all, it stands for High End Audio Rental. I work with brands whose makers have a passion for their craft. Like a good Italian tailored suit: it doesn't need to draw attention, but the connoisseur sees the cut, the materials and the finish.

I'm always trying out new brands and products. I love building relationships with European light and sound manufacturers and working with professionals in the field. I learn a lot from it. It keeps you humble, too: the more you learn from true specialists, the more you notice what you don't know yet.

HEAR also stands for choosing by ear. A rider-friendly range matters commercially, of course, but so does taste. I'm a little headstrong about that. Whatever goes into the HEAR flight case has to suit the artist and the night. It has to be something I stand behind with conviction, made with craftsmanship, respect for the culture, and innovation.
