AUDIENCES

Corporate events

Corporate events come in all shapes and sizes. A staff party for a 100th anniversary. A product demonstration. A seminar or round-table, with microphones for the speakers.

These jobs require a high attention to detail. Keynote speakers who need to be clearly heard and seen. The technology has to suit the room and the brand. Sometimes there is also a need for additional services, such as a livestream or producing and broadcasting a corporate video. And the atmosphere needs to shift smoothly between lectures and celebration.

What we deliver

Complete lighting and sound: speech reinforcement with wireless lavalier microphones, speaker lighting, ambient light and a warm and clear sounding soundsystem for the drinks or the party. DJs and related artists, such as a vocalist, saxophonist or percussionist. Where needed, we of course visit the venue beforehand and tune the sound to the acoustics.

Where music and identity meet

Then there are business occasions that ask for something extra, where identity, technology and music meet. Maarten Versteeg has done this as a DJ for brands for years. Choosing the right music for a catwalk, for instance, agreeing beforehand on the collection and the sound that suits it. Or the music for the launch of a new line of watches in Basel, Switzerland. With 25 years of corporate communications background, we know what brand management is about.

What we deliver

Together with the client or the advertising agency, we translate the identity of the brand or the purpose of the event into a made-to-measure musical approach. For instance the sound for a fashion show, a custom playlist for a networking evening or a DJ set that fits the brand.
