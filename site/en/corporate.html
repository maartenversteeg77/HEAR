Business events: from clear speech reinforcement to music at the afterparty

AUDIENCES

Corporate events

Corporate events come in all shapes and sizes. A staff party for a 100th anniversary. A product demonstration. A seminar or round-table, with microphones for the speakers.

These jobs require a high attention to detail. Keynote speakers who need to be clearly heard and seen. The technology has to suit the room and the brand. And the atmosphere needs to shift smoothly between lectures and celebration.

A tight basic setup or a complete show? That is up to you

For a seminar or networking drinks, a tight basic setup is often enough: clear speech amplification, ambient lighting and music for the drinks. From there you can expand with, for example, a light show, video screens, a livestream, a corporate video or a DJ.

Thinking along earlier, if you want<

On request, Maarten joins earlier in the process. For example to think along about the concept, the run sheet or the translation into light and sound, at the moment it helps your event. If a technician is involved, we schedule a short online briefing on request, so they know beforehand what is going on.

Communication around your event

For the communication around your event you can also come to us: advice on the message, speeches and presentations, invitations, a corporate video or an aftermovie. More about communication advice →

Examples of products and services

  • Ambient lighting speaker lighting and wireless ambient light in the colours of your event

Where music and identity meet

Then there are business occasions that ask for something extra, where identity, technology and music meet. Maarten Versteeg has done this as a DJ for brands for years. Choosing the right music for a catwalk, for instance, agreeing beforehand on the collection and the sound that suits it. Or the music for the launch of a new line of watches in Basel, Switzerland. With 25 years of corporate communications background, we know what brand management is about.

Wat we leveren

Together with the client or the advertising agency, we translate the identity of the brand or the purpose of the event into a made-to-measure musical approach. For instance the sound for a fashion show, a custom playlist for a networking evening or a DJ set that fits the brand.
