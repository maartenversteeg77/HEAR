Brands, stores,
creatives and agencies

Your brand, your product or your catwalk is the starting point. Together, we design the whole experience around it.

We like working with the creatives of the brand or agency. You bring the concept and the brand identity; we translate it into light, sound and music.

That way, a product demo, a shop window or a catwalk becomes a moment that fits the rest of the campaign.

Shaping it together

The moments that make the difference

  • The reveal of a product or collection
  • A demo area with the product at the centre
  • The shop window or the in-store moment
  • The catwalk
  • A photogenic spot or branded backdrop
  • An interview corner for press and content
  • The switch to a party, if the night calls for it

We also offer musical support

The sound of your brand

Music for brands

A brand has its own sound. For products and collections, Maarten brings a refined sound rooted in warm electronic music: subtle enough for a presentation, with enough groove for the night after. Tuned to the collection or the campaign, as a DJ set or as a playlist per moment.

Retail and concept stores

Music for retail

In the store, too, the music belongs to your brand. With an in-store event, a launch or a shopping Sunday with a DJ, your store becomes a place people come for, for an afternoon or evening, with light and sound that fit your collection.
