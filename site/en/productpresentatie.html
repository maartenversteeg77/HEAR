Product launch
with theatre lighting, sound and DJ

THE BRIEF

A fashion brand organised a product presentation with several platforms and a DJ moment. The space had to work for the product presentation by day and invite people to dance later in the evening.

EXPERIENCE

HEAR did a site visit, the lighting design and the programming. A range of spots, designed for theatre and product demonstrations, picked out the sneakers, clothing and accessories on the platforms precisely. Wireless wash lights coloured the space, so no cables were visible anywhere and the venue didn't have to supply extra power. When the lights went down in the evening there was a festive transition, supported by light columns, mirror balls, haze and a disco DJ. Thanks to a laser projector, the new collection stayed visible on the wall all evening.

RESULT

The client described the look they wanted as clean and slightly industrial, without turning cold. A combination of sleek design and warm light achieved exactly that. The DJ was very happy with the latest generation of media players, the monitoring and the PA.
