Communication advice for your event: from message to aftermovie

An event is also communication. What do you want guests to remember afterwards, and how do you make sure the programme supports that? Maarten has worked for some 25 years as a corporate communication specialist for large organisations. He brings that experience alongside light, sound and music, so that technology and message reinforce each other.

Ask about the options→

ADVICE

Thinking along about concept, message and programme

What is the goal of the event, and what should stick? We think along about the structure of the programme, the order of speakers and the transition from content to meeting.

MATERIALS

From invitation to presentation

  • Invitations and other communication materials around the event
  • Speeches and presentations: from storyline to final version
  • A corporate video to show at the event
  • An aftermovie that keeps the event alive afterwards

We make video and photography together with partners from our network.

HOW WE WORK

You engage communication advice through Maarten Versteeg communicatieadvies, separately from hiring light and sound through HEAR. Combining is possible and saves you time on coordination, because message and technology then come from one hand. It is not required. If you choose both, you receive two separate quotes.
