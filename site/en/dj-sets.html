A journey through 40 years of electronic music, from cocktail bar to dancefloor

Want to know more? Watch and listen at maartenversteeg.nl

THE BRIEF

Maarten Versteeg can be booked directly as a DJ and is also affiliated with Architects in Music, which places DJs at special venues and events. From luxury cocktail bars and sky lounges to beach clubs and corporate events.

APPROACH

With deep house as the connecting thread, Maarten plays the soulful side of electronic music (soulful house, garage, disco, latin, afro) and the more abstract side (tech house), depending on what the night calls for.

RESULT

No fuss, no Instagrammable moves: it's the music that is adventurous. The content is the performance. Daring to combine different genres, from classics to new work, shows a connoisseur with a broad knowledge of records and a feel for coherence.
