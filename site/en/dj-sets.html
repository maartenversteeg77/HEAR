A journey through 40 years of electronic music, from cocktail bar to dancefloor

THE BRIEF

Maarten Versteeg can be booked directly as a DJ and is also affiliated with Architects in Music, which books DJs at special venues and events. From luxury cocktail bars and sky lounges to beach clubs and corporate events.

More about booking DJs →

APPROACH

Maarten plays the soulful side of electronic music (soulful house, garage, disco, latin, afro) and the more abstract side (deephouse, tech house), depending on what the night calls for.

RESULT

No fuss, no Instagrammable moves: it's the music that is adventurous. The sound is what makes the performance. To combine different genres, from classics to new releases in a cohesive mix.
