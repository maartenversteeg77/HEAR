Radio mixes for Deep Radio: soulful house, deep house and classics

THE BRIEF

Maarten Versteeg regularly makes DJ mixes on request for radio stations such as Deep Radio. He also makes custom Spotify playlists for cafés and bars, without generic AI sounds.

EXPERIENCE

The mixes move between soulful house, deep house and classics. A selection can be heard on SoundCloud. In future broadcasts Maarten will also introduce his own house productions.

RESULT

Listeners respond strongly to the variety and to the way classics and new material are connected.
