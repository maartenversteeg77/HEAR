Organisers of
dance events

You want your party to sound like a good club. Even when it takes place in a warehouse, a skate hall or a space that's yours for just one night.

As a promoter or collective, you work with a different venue every night, short setup times and DJs with a rider. Throwing your own party? Then you want the same level as the clubs you like to go to yourself.

We make sure the dancefloor works all night: sound that's good everywhere, a booth where every DJ can start playing right away, and light that fits the moment.

Our sound

Even, warm and calculated

A good sound system isn't the loudest one. The sound is spread evenly across the dancefloor: not too loud at the front, not thin at the back. It's full, clear and never harsh, so it stays pleasant for the crowd.

For that, we work with two brands that design and build their entire systems themselves. Dutch brand Brooklyn Audio designs all its cabinets with the same phase response. That means you can combine them any way you like without the sound falling apart, and a small setup sounds the same as a large one. From Italian brand RCF, we use the KX series, the brand's new flagship range, with tops that combine the directness of a point source with the throw of a line source.

The system is modular: the same family of speakers fits a compact room and a hall up to 35 metres deep. We calculate the setup in advance for the dimensions of your room.
