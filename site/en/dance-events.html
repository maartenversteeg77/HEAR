You want your party to sound like a great club, even if it's in a warehouse, a skate hall or a space that's only yours for one night.

As a promoter or collective, you deal with a different venue every time, tight load-in windows and DJs with riders. Throwing your own party? Then you want the same standard as the clubs you love going to.

We make sure the dancefloor works all night long: great sound wherever you stand, a booth every DJ can step into and play, and lighting that matches the moment.

Our sound

Even, warm and tailored to the room

A good sound system isn't the loudest one. The sound is spread evenly across the floor: not too loud at the front, not thin at the back. It's full, clear and never harsh, so it stays enjoyable for the crowd all night.

That's why we work with two brands that design and build their entire systems in-house. Dutch brand Brooklyn Audio gives all its cabinets the same phase response, so you can combine them freely without the sound falling apart, and a small rig sounds just like a big one. From Italy's RCF, we use the KX series, the brand's new flagship line, with tops that combine the punch of a point source with the throw of a line array.

The system is modular: the same speaker family works in a compact room or a hall up to 35 metres deep. We model the setup in advance to fit your space.
