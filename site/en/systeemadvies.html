System advice: a sound system that grows with a rental company

THE BRIEF

A lighting and sound rental company for private events was outgrowing its existing system. More powerful sound was needed, but above all advice on system choice and expansion.

EXPERIENCE

HEAR advised on which type of system suits different rooms, which purchase can grow with the rental company and how to set the system up properly. Maarten also guided the purchase at a sharp price.

RESULT

Good advice reduces the risk of an expensive wrong purchase and helps a rental company choose a system that fits today's jobs and tomorrow's growth.
